Eurasia Capitals Quiz – Type the Capitals

Type the Capitals / Eurasia / 95 countries

Practice capital-city recall while keeping each country tied to its place on the map.

What this quiz covers

Eurasia treats Europe and Asia as one continuous landmass. The quiz reaches from the Atlantic coast to East Asia and includes the transcontinental countries that connect the two conventional continents.

How the capitals quiz works

One country is highlighted and named on the map. Type its capital city, submit your answer, and Smurdy briefly marks the capital's location before moving on through all 95 countries.

Gameplay tip: Use the highlighted country as context, but try to recall the capital before looking for where the answer dot appears.

What you'll practice

This mode connects three facts at once: the country name, its location, and its capital city. The answer dot adds a quick location check after every attempt.

A good way to start

Read the country name, recall its capital without scanning the map, then use the brief answer dot to reinforce where that city actually sits.

When this mode helps

Use this when you know the countries in this set and want to add capital-city recall without losing the geographic context of the map.

One mistake to watch for

Don't automatically type the country's largest or most famous city. Some capitals are smaller or less internationally prominent.

What makes this set challenging

The number of answers is high, and the map includes every kind of regional pattern found in Europe and Asia. Small European states appear beside some of the world's largest countries.

Study tip

Treat Eurasia as several linked regions rather than one list, then practice moving between those regions in the combined quiz.
