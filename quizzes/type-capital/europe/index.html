Type the Capitals / Europe / 44 countries

Practice the capitals of Europe while keeping each country tied to its place on the map.

What this Europe quiz covers

Europe packs a large number of countries into a relatively small map. This set covers Western Europe, Eastern Europe, the Balkans, the Nordic region, the Mediterranean, and the continent's microstates.

How the capitals quiz works

One country is highlighted and named on the map. Type its capital city, submit your answer, and Smurdy briefly marks the capital's location before moving on through all 44 countries.

Gameplay tip: Use the highlighted country as context, but try to recall the capital before looking for where the answer dot appears.

What you'll practice

This mode connects three facts at once: the country name, its location, and its capital city. The answer dot adds a quick location check after every attempt.

A good way to start

Read the country name, recall its capital without scanning the map, then use the brief answer dot to reinforce where that city actually sits.

When this mode helps

Use this when you know the countries in this set and want to add capital-city recall without losing the geographic context of the map.

One mistake to watch for

Don't automatically type the country's largest or most famous city. Some capitals are smaller or less internationally prominent.

What makes this group challenging

Europe has many small neighboring countries, especially around the Balkans and central Europe. Similar names and compact borders make exact placement more demanding than the continent's size suggests.

Study tip

Use the Iberian Peninsula, Italy, the British Isles, and Scandinavia as anchors, then fill in the countries between them.
