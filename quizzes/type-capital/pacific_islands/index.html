Type the Capitals: Pacific Islands Quiz

Type the Capitals / Pacific Islands / 12 countries

Practice the capitals of the Pacific islands while keeping each country tied to its place on the map.

What this Pacific Islands quiz covers

Pacific Islands focuses on the independent countries scattered across the Pacific, including Melanesian, Micronesian, and Polynesian states in Smurdy's group list. Australia and New Zealand are left out so the island nations remain the focus.

How the capitals quiz works

One country is highlighted and named on the map. Type its capital city, submit your answer, and Smurdy briefly marks the capital's location before moving on through all 12 countries.

Use this when you know the countries in this set and want to add capital-city recall without losing the geographic context of the map.

Tip: Use the highlighted country as context, but try to recall the capital before looking for where the answer dot appears.

What makes this group challenging

The countries are separated by huge ocean distances, and several are represented by very small map areas. Relative position is more useful than border shape.
