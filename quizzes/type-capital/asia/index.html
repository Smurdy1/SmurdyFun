Type the Capitals / Asia / 51 countries

Practice the capitals of Asia while keeping each country tied to its place on the map.

What this Asia quiz covers

Asia stretches from the Middle East and Central Asia to India, China, Southeast Asia, and the Pacific coast. The map combines some of the world's largest countries with dense clusters of smaller neighbors.

How the capitals quiz works

One country is highlighted and named on the map. Type its capital city, submit your answer, and Smurdy briefly marks the capital's location before moving on through all 51 countries.

Gameplay tip: Use the highlighted country as context, but try to recall the capital before looking for where the answer dot appears.

What you'll practice

This mode connects three facts at once: the country name, its location, and its capital city. The answer dot adds a quick location check after every attempt.

A good way to start

Read the country name, recall its capital without scanning the map, then use the brief answer dot to reinforce where that city actually sits.

When this mode helps

Use this when you know the countries in this set and want to add capital-city recall without losing the geographic context of the map.

One mistake to watch for

Don't automatically type the country's largest or most famous city. Some capitals are smaller or less internationally prominent.

What makes this group challenging

Central Asia, the Caucasus, and mainland Southeast Asia contain several neighboring countries that are commonly confused. The continent also stretches across a very wide map.

Study tip

Study Asia by subregion before attempting the full quiz, especially South Asia, Central Asia, Southeast Asia, and the Middle East.
