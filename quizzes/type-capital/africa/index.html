Type the Capitals: Africa Quiz

Type the Capitals / Africa / 56 countries

Practice the capitals of Africa while keeping each country tied to its place on the map.

African capitals

Africa contains more than fifty answers in this group, including North Africa, West Africa, the Horn, central Africa, southern Africa, and nearby island countries. It is one of the site's most detailed continent quizzes.

Nairobi and Addis Ababa are familiar anchors; Brazzaville and Kinshasa are a pair worth learning together.

A few easy swaps

The two Congos are especially easy to reverse, and several countries have capitals that are less famous than another city in the same country.

How the capitals quiz works

One country is highlighted and named on the map. Type its capital city, submit your answer, and Smurdy briefly marks the capital's location before moving on through all 56 countries.

Use this when you know the countries in this set and want to add capital-city recall without losing the geographic context of the map.

Tip: Use the highlighted country as context, but try to recall the capital before looking for where the answer dot appears.

One mistake to watch for

Don't automatically type the country's largest or most famous city. Some capitals are smaller or less prominent.

Learn a few anchor countries first

Learn the coastline first, then use those coastal countries to place the landlocked countries behind them.
