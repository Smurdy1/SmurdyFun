Type the Capitals / Americas / 35 countries

Practice the capitals of the Americas while keeping each country tied to its place on the map.

What this Americas quiz covers

The Americas round combines North America, Central America, the Caribbean, and South America. It is a full western hemisphere challenge with both continental countries and small island nations.

How the capitals quiz works

One country is highlighted and named on the map. Type its capital city, submit your answer, and Smurdy briefly marks the capital's location before moving on through all 35 countries.

Gameplay tip: Use the highlighted country as context, but try to recall the capital before looking for where the answer dot appears.

What you'll practice

This mode connects three facts at once: the country name, its location, and its capital city. The answer dot adds a quick location check after every attempt.

A good way to start

Read the country name, recall its capital without scanning the map, then use the brief answer dot to reinforce where that city actually sits.

When this mode helps

Use this when you know the countries in this set and want to add capital-city recall without losing the geographic context of the map.

One mistake to watch for

Don't automatically type the country's largest or most famous city. Some capitals are smaller or less internationally prominent.

What makes this group challenging

The map moves from very large countries to tiny islands, so players must adjust quickly between different scales and regional patterns.

Study tip

Master North America and South America separately, then use this quiz to connect them through Central America and the Caribbean.
