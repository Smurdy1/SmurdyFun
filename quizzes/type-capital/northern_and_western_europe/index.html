What this Northern and Western Europe quiz covers

Northern and Western Europe brings together the Atlantic-facing countries, the British Isles, Scandinavia, the Nordic area, and much of western continental Europe. The map mixes long northern coastlines with compact central borders.

How the capitals quiz works

One country is highlighted and named on the map. Type its capital city, submit your answer, and Smurdy briefly marks the capital's location before moving on through all 19 countries.

Use this when you know the countries in this set and want to add capital-city recall without losing the geographic context of the map.

Tip: Use the highlighted country as context, but try to recall the capital before looking for where the answer dot appears.
