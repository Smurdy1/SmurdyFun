Type the Capitals / Balkans / 12 countries

Practice the capitals of the Balkans while keeping each country tied to its place on the map.

What this Balkans quiz covers

The Balkans occupy southeastern Europe around the Adriatic, Aegean, and western Black Sea region. This focused set is useful because the area has many countries packed into a small space.

How the capitals quiz works

One country is highlighted and named on the map. Type its capital city, submit your answer, and Smurdy briefly marks the capital's location before moving on through all 12 countries.

Gameplay tip: Use the highlighted country as context, but try to recall the capital before looking for where the answer dot appears.

What you'll practice

This mode connects three facts at once: the country name, its location, and its capital city. The answer dot adds a quick location check after every attempt.

A good way to start

Read the country name, recall its capital without scanning the map, then use the brief answer dot to reinforce where that city actually sits.

When this mode helps

Use this when you know the countries in this set and want to add capital-city recall without losing the geographic context of the map.

One mistake to watch for

Don't automatically type the country's largest or most famous city. Some capitals are smaller or less prominent.

What makes this group challenging

Several countries are small, landlocked, or have similar shapes. The exact cultural definition of the Balkans can also differ, so this quiz follows the country list stored in Smurdy.

Study tip

Use Greece, Croatia, Romania, and Bulgaria as outer anchors, then place the smaller central countries between them.

Example countries

  • Romania
  • Republic of Serbia

Countries included in this quiz (12)

Albania, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Bulgaria, Croatia, Greece, Kosovo, Montenegro, North Macedonia, Republic of Serbia, Romania, Slovenia, Turkey.
