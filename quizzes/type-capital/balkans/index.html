Type the Capitals: Balkans Quiz

Type the Capitals / Balkans / 12 countries

Practice the capitals of the Balkans while keeping each country tied to its place on the map.

Small borders, similar neighbors

The Balkans occupy southeastern Europe around the Adriatic, Aegean, and western Black Sea region. This focused set is useful because the area has many countries packed into a small space.

Slovenia, Croatia, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Serbia, Montenegro, and North Macedonia sit close enough that a rough sense of direction is not enough.

Names are only half the problem

The Balkans are a good test of whether you know the actual order of neighboring countries instead of only recognizing the names as a regional cluster.

How the capitals quiz works

One country is highlighted and named on the map. Type its capital city, submit your answer, and Smurdy briefly marks the capital's location before moving on through all 12 countries.

Use this when you know the countries in this set and want to add capital-city recall without losing the geographic context of the map.

Tip: Use the highlighted country as context, but try to recall the capital before looking for where the answer dot appears.

A few kilometers on screen can matter

Several countries are small, landlocked, or have similar shapes. The exact cultural definition of the Balkans can also differ, so this quiz follows the country list stored in Smurdy.
