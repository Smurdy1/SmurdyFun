Type the Capitals: Sub-Saharan Africa Quiz

Type the Capitals / Sub-Saharan Africa / 49 countries

Practice the capitals of Sub-Saharan Africa while keeping each country tied to its place on the map.

What this Sub-Saharan Africa quiz covers

Sub-Saharan Africa contains the countries south of the Sahara according to Smurdy's stored group list. Removing North Africa still leaves a large map spanning West, Central, East, and Southern Africa.

How the capitals quiz works

One country is highlighted and named on the map. Type its capital city, submit your answer, and Smurdy briefly marks the capital's location before moving on through all 49 countries.

Use this when you know the countries in this set and want to add capital-city recall without losing the geographic context of the map.

Tip: Use the highlighted country as context, but try to recall the capital before looking for where the answer dot appears.
