Type the Capitals: Eastern Europe Quiz

Type the Capitals / Eastern Europe / 10 countries

Practice the capitals of Eastern Europe while keeping each country tied to its place on the map.

A compact region with few easy coastlines

Eastern Europe provides a smaller practice set for the countries on the eastern side of the European map. The exact regional boundary varies by source, so Smurdy follows the consistent country list stored in its group file.

Belarus, Ukraine, Moldova, Romania, and Bulgaria are easier when you remember who touches whom than when you search for one distinctive outline.

How the capitals quiz works

One country is highlighted and named on the map. Type its capital city, submit your answer, and Smurdy briefly marks the capital's location before moving on through all 10 countries.

Use this when you know the countries in this set and want to add capital-city recall without losing the geographic context of the map.

Tip: Use the highlighted country as context, but try to recall the capital before looking for where the answer dot appears.

Trace the region from north to south

Place the Baltic area, Poland, Ukraine, and the Black Sea first, then fill the countries between those landmarks.
