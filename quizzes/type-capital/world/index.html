Type the World Capitals Quiz

Type the Capitals / World / 201 countries

Practice the capitals of the world while keeping each country tied to its place on the map.

201 capitals, one map

The full world map brings together countries from every continent, from the largest continental states to small island nations. The round tests country names, locations, shapes, and regional context instead of concentrating on one part of the globe.

A round can move from Ottawa to Abuja, then Bishkek, Suva, and Montevideo.

Why World feels different

A continent quiz lets you keep one mental map loaded. World keeps making you throw that map away and load another one, which is why familiar countries can suddenly take a second longer.

How this one works

One country is highlighted and named on the map. Type its capital city, submit your answer, and Smurdy briefly marks the capital's location before moving on through all 201 countries.

Use this when you know the countries in this set and want to add capital-city recall without losing the geographic context of the map.

Tip: Use the highlighted country as context, but try to recall the capital before looking for where the answer dot appears.

One mistake to watch for

Don't automatically type the country's largest or most famous city. Some capitals are smaller or less prominent.

Where World usually bites back

The hardest part is switching quickly between very different parts of the world. Small countries in Europe, the Caribbean, and the Pacific can also be easy to overlook.
