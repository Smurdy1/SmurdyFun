Type the Capitals / Oceania / 14 countries

Practice the capitals of Oceania while keeping each country tied to its place on the map.

What this Oceania quiz covers

Oceania covers Australia, New Zealand, Papua New Guinea, and the independent island countries of the Pacific. Unlike a continuous continental map, this group is built around widely separated land areas.

How the capitals quiz works

One country is highlighted and named on the map. Type its capital city, submit your answer, and Smurdy briefly marks the capital's location before moving on through all 14 countries.

Gameplay tip: Use the highlighted country as context, but try to recall the capital before looking for where the answer dot appears.

What you'll practice

This mode connects three facts at once: the country name, its location, and its capital city. The answer dot adds a quick location check after every attempt.

A good way to start

Read the country name, recall its capital without scanning the map, then use the brief answer dot to reinforce where that city actually sits.

When this mode helps

Use this when you know the countries in Oceania and want to add capital-city recall without losing the geographic context of the map.

One mistake to watch for

Don't automatically type the country's largest or most famous city. Some capitals are smaller or less internationally prominent.

What makes this group challenging

Many countries are small and far apart, so relative position matters more than shared land borders. Several island nations can look like isolated points at a world scale.

Study tip

Use Australia, New Zealand, and Papua New Guinea as anchors, then learn the island countries by their position north, east, or northeast of those anchors.
