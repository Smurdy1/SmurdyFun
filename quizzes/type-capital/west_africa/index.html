Type the Capitals: West Africa Quiz

Type the Capitals / West Africa / 16 countries

Practice the capitals of West Africa while keeping each country tied to its place on the map.

A compact coast with a wide Sahel

West Africa runs from the Atlantic coast into the Sahel and the inland countries behind the Gulf of Guinea. The group includes Senegal, Mali, Ghana, Nigeria, Ivory Coast, and many smaller neighbors.

Senegal, Ghana, Niger, and Nigeria make useful reference points, while the smaller coastal states reward learning the exact west-to-east order.

How the capitals quiz works

One country is highlighted and named on the map. Type its capital city, submit your answer, and Smurdy briefly marks the capital's location before moving on through all 16 countries.

Use this when you know the countries in this set and want to add capital-city recall without losing the geographic context of the map.

Tip: Use the highlighted country as context, but try to recall the capital before looking for where the answer dot appears.

The coastal sequence gets crowded

The coastline contains many smaller countries packed between larger neighbors, while several inland states have similar broad shapes.
