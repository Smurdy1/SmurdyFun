South America Capitals Quiz – Type the Capitals

Type the Capitals / South America / 12 countries

Practice the capitals of South America while keeping each country tied to its place on the map.

What this quiz covers

South America has a manageable number of countries and several clear coastal shapes. The group runs from Colombia and Venezuela in the north to Argentina and Chile in the south.

How the capitals quiz works

One country is highlighted and named on the map. Type its capital city, submit your answer, and Smurdy briefly marks the capital's location before moving on through all 12 countries.

Gameplay tip: Use the highlighted country as context, but try to recall the capital before looking for where the answer dot appears.

What you'll practice

This mode connects three facts at once: the country name, its location, and its capital city. The answer dot adds a quick location check after every attempt.

A good way to start

Read the country name, recall its capital without scanning the map, then use the brief answer dot to reinforce where that city actually sits.

When this mode helps

Use this when you know the countries in this set and want to add capital-city recall without losing the geographic context of the map.

One mistake to watch for

Don't automatically type the country's largest or most famous city. Some capitals are smaller or less internationally prominent.

What makes this set challenging

The Guianas in the northeast and the inland positions of Bolivia and Paraguay are the places most likely to cause mistakes.

Study tip

Use Brazil as the central anchor, then learn which countries touch its long border and which do not.

Example countries

  • Brazil
  • Argentina
  • Colombia
  • Chile
  • Peru
Countries included in this quiz (12)

Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil, Chile, Colombia, Ecuador, Guyana, Paraguay, Peru, Suriname, Uruguay, Venezuela.
