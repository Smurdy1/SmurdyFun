Middle East Capitals Quiz – Type the Capitals

Type the Capitals / Middle East / 17 countries

Practice the capitals of the Middle East while keeping each country tied to its place on the map.

What this quiz covers

The Middle East group centers on the eastern Mediterranean, the Arabian Peninsula, Iran, Iraq, and nearby parts of western Asia. It gives players a focused alternative to practicing the whole Asian continent at once.

How the capitals quiz works

One country is highlighted and named on the map. Type its capital city, submit your answer, and Smurdy briefly marks the capital's location before moving on through all 17 countries.

Gameplay tip: Use the highlighted country as context, but try to recall the capital before looking for where the answer dot appears.

What you'll practice

This mode connects three facts at once: the country name, its location, and its capital city. The answer dot adds a quick location check after every attempt.

A good way to start

Read the country name, recall its capital without scanning the map, then use the brief answer dot to reinforce where that city actually sits.

When this mode helps

Use this when you know the countries in this set and want to add capital-city recall without losing the geographic context of the map.

One mistake to watch for

Don't automatically type the country's largest or most famous city. Some capitals are smaller or less internationally prominent.

What makes this set challenging

The Levant and Persian Gulf contain several small neighboring countries, while the boundaries between regional definitions can vary between sources.

Study tip

Learn the Arabian Peninsula as one shape, then place the Levant, Iraq, and Iran around it.
