What this North America quiz covers

This set combines Canada, the United States, Mexico, Central America, and the sovereign Caribbean countries included in Smurdy's North America group.

How the flag quiz works

One flag appears at a time. Type the country it represents, or choose Give Up to reveal the answer. The round continues through all 23 countries.

Gameplay tip: Check the stripe direction, color order, symbols, and proportions before answering.

What you'll practice

This mode builds visual recognition and name recall together. You have to produce the full place name instead of choosing it from a list.

A good way to start

Identify the broad design family first, then use the smallest distinctive feature to separate it from similar flags.

What makes this set challenging

The Caribbean contributes many smaller countries whose flag designs may be less familiar than the three large mainland countries.

Study tip

Learn the mainland and Central America first, then study the Caribbean flags as a separate cluster.
