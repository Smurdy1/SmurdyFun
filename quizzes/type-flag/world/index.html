Type the World Flags Quiz

Type the Flags / World / 201 flags

Identify every country and territory flag by typing the place it represents.

This complete set mixes flags from every part of the world, including countries, territories, and the other places used by Smurdy's world map quiz.

A single round can jump from Japan to Brazil, then Ghana, Estonia, and Samoa.

How the flag quiz works

One flag appears at a time. Type the country or territory it represents, or choose Give Up to reveal the answer. The round continues through all 201 countries and territories.

Tip: Check the stripe direction, color order, symbols, and proportions before answering.

Why World feels different

A continent quiz lets you keep one mental map loaded. World keeps making you throw that map away and load another one, which is why familiar countries can suddenly take a second longer.

Where World usually bites back

Similar color patterns repeat across continents, and several flags differ only by a symbol, stripe order, or shade.
