All 50 state flags

The set includes every current state flag, from Alabama through Wyoming, including Mississippi's current magnolia flag.

California, Maryland, New Mexico, Colorado, and Alaska stand out quickly; many seal-on-blue flags do not.

How the flag quiz works

One flag appears at a time. Type the state it represents, or choose Give Up to reveal the answer. The round continues through all 50 states.

Tip: Check the stripe direction, color order, symbols, and proportions before answering.

The seal-on-blue problem

A large part of the difficulty comes from states whose flags use a detailed seal on a blue field. Those are better learned from the seal or lettering than from the overall layout.

Learn states in regional blocks

Learn the most distinctive designs first, then compare the similar blue-and-seal flags side by side.
