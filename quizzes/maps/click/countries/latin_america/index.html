Click the Countries: Latin America Map Quiz

Click the Countries / Latin America / 21 countries

Learn the Latin America map by clicking each country when its name appears.

Several map patterns under one label

Latin America stretches from Mexico through Central America and the Caribbean into South America. It is a language-and-history grouping spread across a huge area.

Mexico leads into one long mainland chain. Cuba is an island problem. South America takes up most of the map below them.

How the click map quiz works

A country name appears above the map. Click it and keep going until the set is finished. The borders stay on the map, which makes this the easiest map mode to learn from.

For a map you barely know, this is usually the easiest place to start. It also works well as a refresher after leaving a region alone for a while.

Tip: If one answer keeps getting you, remember one neighbor or coastline beside it. That is usually enough to find it next time.

Split the set by geography

South America and Central America are easier to learn on their own. Add the Caribbean after those two are solid.
