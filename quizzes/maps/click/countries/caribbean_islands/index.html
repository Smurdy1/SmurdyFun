Click the Countries: Caribbean Islands Map Quiz

Click the Countries / Caribbean Islands / 13 countries

Learn the Caribbean Islands map by clicking each country when its name appears.

Tiny targets across a wide sea

The Caribbean set focuses on the sovereign island countries in and around the Caribbean Sea. Cuba and Hispaniola are easy to see; many of the smaller islands are not.

Cuba gives you plenty to click. Dominica and Saint Lucia do not.

Island order matters

The Lesser Antilles get much easier once the north-to-south order feels familiar. At world zoom, the individual island shapes are barely useful.

How the click map quiz works

A country name appears above the map. Click it and keep going until the set is finished. The borders stay on the map, which makes this the easiest map mode to learn from.

For a map you barely know, this is usually the easiest place to start. It also works well as a refresher after leaving a region alone for a while.

Tip: If one answer keeps getting you, remember one neighbor or coastline beside it. That is usually enough to find it next time.

The eastern chain is the real test

Several answers are tiny at normal zoom, and some nearby islands are territories rather than sovereign-country answers.
