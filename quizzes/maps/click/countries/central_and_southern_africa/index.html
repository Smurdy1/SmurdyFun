Click the Countries: Central and Southern Africa Map Quiz

Click the Countries / Central and Southern Africa / 18 countries

Learn the Central and Southern Africa map by clicking each country when its name appears.

Big interiors and a crowded south

This set runs from the Congo Basin down to the southern end of Africa. It includes a mix of very large inland countries and several compact states around South Africa.

The Democratic Republic of the Congo, Zambia, Botswana, Lesotho, and Eswatini create very different scale problems in the same part of the map.

How the click map quiz works

A country name appears above the map. Click it and keep going until the set is finished. The borders stay on the map, which makes this the easiest map mode to learn from.

For a map you barely know, this is usually the easiest place to start. It also works well as a refresher after leaving a region alone for a while.

Tip: If one answer keeps getting you, remember one neighbor or coastline beside it. That is usually enough to find it next time.
