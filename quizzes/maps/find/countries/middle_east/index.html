Find the Countries: Middle East No-Borders Quiz

Find the Countries / Middle East / 16 countries

Test your knowledge of the Middle East by finding each country on a map with borders hidden.

Small Gulf states beside very large neighbors

This group covers the eastern Mediterranean, the Arabian Peninsula, Iran, Iraq and nearby western Asia. It is a much smaller set than all of Asia.

Turkey, Iran and Saudi Arabia are hard to miss. Bahrain can be tiny even after you know exactly where to look.

How the no-borders map quiz works

A country name appears on a map with the political borders hidden. Click where it belongs. The coastline stays, so you still have some geography to work with.

Borderless mode is a good follow-up once the normal click map has become comfortable.

Tip: Pick out a coastline, peninsula or big country you know and judge the target from there.

Scale is the main trap

The Gulf and the Levant have several small neighbors. A tiny shift on the map can put you in the wrong country.
