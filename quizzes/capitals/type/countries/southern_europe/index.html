Type the Capitals: Southern Europe Quiz

Type the Capitals / Southern Europe / 18 countries

Practice the capitals of Southern Europe while keeping each country tied to its place on the map.

Peninsulas, islands, and microstates

Southern Europe is built around the Mediterranean-facing part of the continent: Iberia, Italy, Greece, nearby islands and the smaller states in the region.

Spain, Italy and Greece are easy to orient around. Malta, the microstates and the Balkans need much closer map memory.

How the capitals quiz works

A country is highlighted and named. Type its capital. Smurdy marks the capital on the map for a moment before the next question.

Capital mode makes the most sense once the countries or states themselves are familiar.

Tip: Try to get the city from memory before you look for the answer dot.
