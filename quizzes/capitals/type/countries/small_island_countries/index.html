Type the Capitals: Small Island Countries Quiz

Type the Capitals / Small Island Countries / 27 countries

Practice the capitals of small island countries while keeping each country tied to its place on the map.

The countries most likely to disappear at world zoom

This set pulls small sovereign island countries from several oceans and regions. Most of them show up as tiny shapes or points on the world map.

Malta is tiny but sits in a familiar sea. Nauru gives you much less nearby geography to work with.

How the capitals quiz works

A country is highlighted and named. Type its capital. Smurdy marks the capital on the map for a moment before the next question.

Capital mode makes the most sense once the countries or states themselves are familiar.

Tip: Try to get the city from memory before you look for the answer dot.

Give the hardest islands some local context

Ocean and subregion groups make this set much less random than one giant list.
