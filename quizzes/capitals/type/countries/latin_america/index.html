Type the Capitals: Latin America Quiz

Type the Capitals / Latin America / 21 countries

Practice the capitals of Latin America while keeping each country tied to its place on the map.

Several map patterns under one label

Latin America stretches from Mexico through Central America and the Caribbean into South America. It is a language-and-history grouping spread across a huge area.

Mexico, Cuba, Brazil, and Chile all belong here, so the quiz moves between mainland chains, islands, and an entire continent.

How the capitals quiz works

A country is highlighted and named. Type its capital. Smurdy marks the capital on the map for a moment before the next question.

Capital mode makes the most sense once the countries or states themselves are familiar.

Tip: Try to get the city from memory before you look for the answer dot.

Break it into three chunks

South America and Central America are easier to learn on their own. Add the Caribbean after those two are solid.
