Type the Capitals: Eurasia Quiz

Type the Capitals / Eurasia / 95 countries

Practice the capitals of Eurasia while keeping each country tied to its place on the map.

From the Atlantic edge to the Pacific

Eurasia puts Europe and Asia on one continuous map. It is a huge set, from the Atlantic edge to the Pacific, with every kind of country size in between.

Portugal and Japan can appear in the same round. There are a lot of different maps hiding between them.

How the capitals quiz works

A country is highlighted and named. Type its capital. Smurdy marks the capital on the map for a moment before the next question.

Capital mode makes the most sense once the countries or states themselves are familiar.

Tip: Try to get the city from memory before you look for the answer dot.
