Type the Capitals: Sub-Saharan Africa Quiz

Type the Capitals / Sub-Saharan Africa / 49 countries

Practice the capitals of Sub-Saharan Africa while keeping each country tied to its place on the map.

A very large set with few obvious shortcuts

This is the large Africa set with North Africa removed. It still spans the west, center, east and south of the continent.

Senegal and South Africa give you distant ends of the set. The inland countries between them are where memory gets tested.

How the capitals quiz works

A country is highlighted and named. Type its capital. Smurdy marks the capital on the map for a moment before the next question.

Capital mode makes the most sense once the countries or states themselves are familiar.

Tip: Try to get the city from memory before you look for the answer dot.
