Test your knowledge of South and Central Asia by finding each country on a map with borders hidden.

Mountains and landlocked neighbors

South and Central Asia links the Indian subcontinent with the landlocked countries between the Caspian Sea, China, Russia, and Afghanistan. The combined set shows how the two neighboring regions fit together.

Kazakhstan and India are strong anchors, but Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan, Nepal, Bhutan, and Bangladesh compress a lot of geography into narrow spaces.

How the no-borders map quiz works

The quiz names a country, but the usual political borders are removed. Click where you think it belongs by using coastlines, neighboring landforms, distance, and your memory of the regional layout. This set covers all 13 countries.

Move to this mode when the normal South and Central Asia click quiz starts to feel easy and you want to see whether you actually know where everything goes.

Tip: Use coastlines and the positions of nearby large countries as anchors before judging smaller inland areas.

Build a west-to-east sequence

Use India and Kazakhstan as the two main anchors, then learn the countries surrounding each one.
