Find the Countries / Southern Europe / 18 countries

Test your knowledge of Southern Europe by finding each country on a map with borders hidden.

What this Southern Europe quiz covers

Southern Europe centers on the Mediterranean-facing countries of the Iberian Peninsula, Italy, the Balkans, and nearby microstates included in the group. It gives concentrated practice for the lower half of the European map.

How the no-borders map quiz works

The quiz names a country, but the usual political borders are removed. Click where you think it belongs by using coastlines, neighboring landforms, distance, and your memory of the regional layout. This set covers all 18 countries.

Gameplay tip: Use coastlines and the positions of nearby large countries as anchors before judging smaller inland areas.

What you'll practice

With the border lines gone, you have to remember the layout itself. Coastlines, distances, and the order of nearby places become much more important.

A good way to start

Start from something you know: a coastline, peninsula, island, or large country. Place the target relative to it instead of guessing from empty space.

When this mode helps

Move to this mode when the normal Southern Europe click quiz starts to feel easy and you want to see whether you actually know where everything goes.

One mistake to watch for

The map may look blank, but it still has clues. Use coastlines, peninsulas, large rivers, and countries you already know.

What makes this group challenging

The region combines familiar peninsulas with several very small countries and tightly packed Balkan borders.

Study tip

Learn the three major peninsulas first, then add the island countries and microstates around them.
