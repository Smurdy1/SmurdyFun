Find the Countries: Southern Europe No-Borders Quiz

Find the Countries / Southern Europe / 18 countries

Test your knowledge of Southern Europe by finding each country on a map with borders hidden.

Peninsulas, islands, and microstates

Southern Europe centers on the Mediterranean-facing countries of the Iberian Peninsula, Italy, the Balkans, and nearby microstates included in the group. It gives concentrated practice for the lower half of the European map.

Spain, Italy, and Greece are excellent anchors, but Malta, San Marino, Vatican City, and the Balkans make the small-scale details matter.

How the no-borders map quiz works

The quiz names a country, but the usual political borders are removed. Click where you think it belongs by using coastlines, neighboring landforms, distance, and your memory of the regional layout. This set covers all 18 countries.

Move to this mode when the normal Southern Europe click quiz starts to feel easy and you want to see whether you actually know where everything goes.

Tip: Use coastlines and the positions of nearby large countries as anchors before judging smaller inland areas.
