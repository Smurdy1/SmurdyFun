Test your knowledge of Northern and Western Europe by finding each country on a map with borders hidden.

Coasts, islands, and a dense lowland core

Northern and Western Europe brings together the Atlantic-facing countries, the British Isles, Scandinavia, the Nordic area, and much of western continental Europe. The map mixes long northern coastlines with compact central borders.

The United Kingdom and Scandinavia provide strong outlines, while Benelux and nearby small countries demand much tighter map memory.

How the no-borders map quiz works

The quiz names a country, but the usual political borders are removed. Click where you think it belongs by using coastlines, neighboring landforms, distance, and your memory of the regional layout. This set covers all 19 countries.

Move to this mode when the normal Northern and Western Europe click quiz starts to feel easy and you want to see whether you actually know where everything goes.

Tip: Use coastlines and the positions of nearby large countries as anchors before judging smaller inland areas.
