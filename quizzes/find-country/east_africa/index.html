Test your knowledge of East Africa by finding each country on a map with borders hidden.

What this East Africa quiz covers

East Africa in this group includes the Horn, the Great Lakes region, the Indian Ocean coast, and nearby island countries. Ethiopia, Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, and Somalia provide several recognizable anchors.

How the no-borders map quiz works

The quiz names a country, but the usual political borders are removed. Click where you think it belongs by using coastlines, neighboring landforms, distance, and your memory of the regional layout. This set covers all 17 countries.

Gameplay tip: Use coastlines and the positions of nearby large countries as anchors before judging smaller inland areas.

What you'll practice

With the border lines gone, you have to remember the layout itself. Coastlines, distances, and the order of nearby places become much more important.

A good way to start

Start from something you know: a coastline, peninsula, island, or large country. Place the target relative to it instead of guessing from empty space.

When this mode helps

Move to this mode when the normal East Africa click quiz starts to feel easy and you want to see whether you actually know where everything goes.

One mistake to watch for

The map may look blank, but it still has clues. Use coastlines, peninsulas, large rivers, and countries you already know.

What makes this group challenging

The Horn has distinctive coastlines, but the inland Great Lakes countries are smaller and closely grouped. Regional definitions can also differ near central and southern Africa.

Study tip

Begin with the Horn and Indian Ocean coastline, then work inland around Lake Victoria and the Great Lakes.
