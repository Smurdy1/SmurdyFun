Find the Countries: European Union No-Borders Quiz

Find the Countries / European Union / 27 countries

Test your knowledge of the European Union by finding each country on a map with borders hidden.

A political set, not a geographic one

The European Union group contains the current EU member states as both a political and geographic set. It helps separate EU countries from nearby non-members such as Norway, Switzerland, the United Kingdom, and several Balkan states.

Portugal, Finland, Cyprus, and Ireland belong together here because of membership, not because they form one neat region on the map.

How the no-borders map quiz works

The quiz names a country, but the usual political borders are removed. Click where you think it belongs by using coastlines, neighboring landforms, distance, and your memory of the regional layout. This set covers all 27 countries.

Move to this mode when the normal European Union click quiz starts to feel easy and you want to see whether you actually know where everything goes.

Tip: Use coastlines and the positions of nearby large countries as anchors before judging smaller inland areas.
