Find the Countries: North America No-Borders Quiz

Find the Countries / North America / 23 countries

Test your knowledge of North America by finding each country on a map with borders hidden.

From Canada to the Caribbean

North America in this quiz includes Canada, the United States, Mexico, Central America, and the sovereign Caribbean countries stored in the group. Three very large mainland countries share the map with many smaller tropical states.

Canada and Mexico dominate the land area, but Central America and the island countries create most of the close calls.

How the no-borders map quiz works

The quiz names a country, but the usual political borders are removed. Click where you think it belongs by using coastlines, neighboring landforms, distance, and your memory of the regional layout. This set covers all 23 countries.

Move to this mode when the normal North America click quiz starts to feel easy and you want to see whether you actually know where everything goes.

Tip: Use coastlines and the positions of nearby large countries as anchors before judging smaller inland areas.
