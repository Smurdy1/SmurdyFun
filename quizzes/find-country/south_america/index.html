South America Without Borders Quiz: Find 12 Countries

Find the Countries / South America / 12 countries

Find all 12 sovereign countries of South America with the internal borders removed. Use the continent's coastline, Brazil's central position, the Andes, and your memory of neighboring countries to place every answer.

The 12 countries in this borderless South America quiz

The set includes every sovereign country in South America, from Colombia and Venezuela in the north to Chile and Argentina in the south. Brazil dominates the center and east, while Bolivia and Paraguay form the continent's two landlocked answers.

Why French Guiana is not an answer

French Guiana appears on the northeastern coast between Suriname and Brazil, but it is an overseas department of France rather than a sovereign country. This quiz therefore includes Guyana and Suriname among its 12 answers, but not French Guiana.

How the no-borders map quiz works

A South American country name appears on a map with no internal political lines. Click its approximate location using the outer coastline and your mental map, then continue until all 12 sovereign countries are complete.

Gameplay tip: Use Brazil as the central reference, then decide whether the target lies along the Pacific, Atlantic, Caribbean, or inland.

What you'll practice

With the border lines gone, you have to remember the layout itself. Coastlines, distances, and the order of nearby places become much more important.

A good way to start

Start from something you know: a coastline, peninsula, island, or large country. Place the target relative to it instead of guessing from empty space.

When this mode helps

Move to this mode when the normal South America click quiz starts to feel easy and you want to see whether you actually know where everything goes.

One mistake to watch for

The map may look blank, but it still has clues. Use coastlines, peninsulas, large rivers, and countries you already know.

Most confusing locations without borders

Uruguay and Paraguay can be confused near southern Brazil, while Colombia and Venezuela divide the northern coast. The three Guianas create another trap because only Guyana and Suriname are sovereign-country answers in this quiz.

How to memorize the blank South America map

Learn the five Pacific-side countries from north to south, then the northern Atlantic coast, the Southern Cone, and the two landlocked countries. Finish by checking every answer against Brazil, which borders most of the continent.
