Test your knowledge of World by finding each country on a map with borders hidden.

What this World quiz covers

The full world map brings together countries from every continent, from the largest continental states to small island nations. The round tests country names, locations, shapes, and regional context instead of concentrating on one part of the globe.

How the no-borders map quiz works

The quiz names a country, but the usual political borders are removed. Click where you think it belongs by using coastlines, neighboring landforms, distance, and your memory of the regional layout. This set covers all 201 countries.

Gameplay tip: Use coastlines and the positions of nearby large countries as anchors before judging smaller inland areas.

What you'll practice

With the border lines gone, you have to remember the layout itself. Coastlines, distances, and the order of nearby places become much more important.

A good way to start

Start from something you know: a coastline, peninsula, island, or large country. Place the target relative to it instead of guessing from empty space.

When this mode helps

Move to this mode when the normal World click quiz starts to feel easy and you want to see whether you actually know where everything goes.

One mistake to watch for

The map may look blank, but it still has clues. Use coastlines, peninsulas, large rivers, and countries you already know.

What makes this group challenging

The hardest part is switching quickly between very different parts of the world. Small countries in Europe, the Caribbean, and the Pacific can also be easy to overlook.

Study tip

Learn one continent at a time, then return to the world quiz to practice changing regions without warning.
