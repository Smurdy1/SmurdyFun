Test your knowledge of Americas by finding each country on a map with borders hidden.

What this Americas quiz covers

The Americas round combines North America, Central America, the Caribbean, and South America. It is a full western hemisphere challenge with both continental countries and small island nations.

How the no-borders map quiz works

The quiz names a country, but the usual political borders are removed. Click where you think it belongs by using coastlines, neighboring landforms, distance, and your memory of the regional layout. This set covers all 35 countries.

Gameplay tip: Use coastlines and the positions of nearby large countries as anchors before judging smaller inland areas.

What you'll practice

With the border lines gone, you have to remember the layout itself. Coastlines, distances, and the order of nearby places become much more important.

A good way to start

Start from something you know: a coastline, peninsula, island, or large country. Place the target relative to it instead of guessing from empty space.

When this mode helps

Move to this mode when the normal Americas click quiz starts to feel easy and you want to see whether you actually know where everything goes.

One mistake to watch for

The map may look blank, but it still has clues. Use coastlines, peninsulas, large rivers, and countries you already know.

What makes this group challenging

The map moves from very large countries to tiny islands, so players must adjust quickly between different scales and regional patterns.

Study tip

Master North America and South America separately, then use this quiz to connect them through Central America and the Caribbean.
