Asia Without Borders Quiz: Find All 51 Countries

Find the Countries / Asia / 51 countries

Can you find all 51 countries of Asia with every political border removed? Use coastlines, islands, peninsulas, and relative position to complete this blank-map geography quiz.

Countries included in the Asia without-borders quiz

This quiz covers 51 Asian countries across the Middle East, Central Asia, South Asia, Southeast Asia, East Asia, and the continent's transcontinental edges. The full set stretches from Turkey and Cyprus to Japan, Indonesia, and Timor-Leste.

Build an Asian coastline framework

Before learning exact inland positions, trace Asia's outer shape from the eastern Mediterranean around Arabia and India, through Southeast Asia, and up the Pacific coast. Add Japan, the Philippines, Indonesia, and Sri Lanka as island anchors. That framework gives every landlocked country a more precise place in your mental map.

How the no-borders map quiz works

A country name appears while the usual political outlines stay hidden. Click its approximate location on the blank map, then continue through all 51 countries. Unlike the standard Asia click quiz, this version tests whether you know the continent's underlying layout without matching visible border shapes.

Move to this mode when the normal Asia click quiz starts to feel easy and you want to see whether you actually know where everything goes.

Tip: Start with a coastline, island chain, or major peninsula, then estimate how far inland the target sits.

Hardest parts of a borderless Asia map

Central Asia and the Caucasus are difficult because their countries are inland and packed between a few large anchors. Mainland Southeast Asia creates a different problem: narrow countries sit close together along complex coastlines and peninsulas.
