Find the Countries: Latin America No-Borders Quiz

Find the Countries / Latin America / 21 countries

Test your knowledge of Latin America by finding each country on a map with borders hidden.

Several map patterns under one label

Latin America connects Mexico, Central America, much of the Caribbean, and South America in one broad regional set. The grouping mainly follows shared Spanish, Portuguese, and French colonial language history.

Mexico, Cuba, Brazil, and Chile all belong here, so the quiz moves between mainland chains, islands, and an entire continent.

How the no-borders map quiz works

The quiz names a country, but the usual political borders are removed. Click where you think it belongs by using coastlines, neighboring landforms, distance, and your memory of the regional layout. This set covers all 21 countries.

Move to this mode when the normal Latin America click quiz starts to feel easy and you want to see whether you actually know where everything goes.

Tip: Use coastlines and the positions of nearby large countries as anchors before judging smaller inland areas.

Break it into three chunks

Learn Central America and South America separately, then add the Caribbean countries included in the group.
