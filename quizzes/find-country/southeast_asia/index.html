Find the Countries / Southeast Asia / 11 countries

Test your knowledge of Southeast Asia by finding each country on a map with borders hidden.

What this Southeast Asia quiz covers

Southeast Asia includes the mainland countries between India and China along with the major island states to the south and east. Thailand, Vietnam, Indonesia, the Philippines, and Malaysia are among the best-known landmarks in the group.

How the no-borders map quiz works

The quiz names a country, but the usual political borders are removed. Click where you think it belongs by using coastlines, neighboring landforms, distance, and your memory of the regional layout. This set covers all 11 countries.

Gameplay tip: Use coastlines and the positions of nearby large countries as anchors before judging smaller inland areas.

What you'll practice

With the border lines gone, you have to remember the layout itself. Coastlines, distances, and the order of nearby places become much more important.

A good way to start

Start from something you know: a coastline, peninsula, island, or large country. Place the target relative to it instead of guessing from empty space.

When this mode helps

Move to this mode when the normal Southeast Asia click quiz starts to feel easy and you want to see whether you actually know where everything goes.

One mistake to watch for

The map may look blank, but it still has clues. Use coastlines, peninsulas, large rivers, and countries you already know.

What makes this group challenging

Mainland borders are tightly packed, while Indonesia and the Philippines are spread across many islands. Malaysia is also split between the Malay Peninsula and Borneo.

Study tip

Study mainland Southeast Asia from west to east, then learn the island countries as a second map pattern.
