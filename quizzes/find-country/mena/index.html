Test your knowledge of the Middle East and North Africa by finding each country on a map with borders hidden.

A band from Morocco to Iran

MENA, short for Middle East and North Africa, stretches from Morocco and Algeria to Iran and the Arabian Peninsula. The group crosses Africa and Asia while keeping together countries that are often studied as one region.

Morocco, Egypt, Saudi Arabia, and Iran are strong anchors; the smaller states around the eastern Mediterranean and Gulf make the transitions harder.

Two different map problems

North Africa is mostly a west-to-east sequence, while the Middle East depends much more on compact border relationships. Treating them the same usually makes the set harder.

How the no-borders map quiz works

The quiz names a country, but the usual political borders are removed. Click where you think it belongs by using coastlines, neighboring landforms, distance, and your memory of the regional layout. This set covers all 21 countries.

Move to this mode when the normal Middle East and North Africa click quiz starts to feel easy and you want to see whether you actually know where everything goes.

Tip: Use coastlines and the positions of nearby large countries as anchors before judging smaller inland areas.

Learn North Africa and the Middle East separately

Learn North Africa west to east, then attach the Levant, Arabian Peninsula, Iraq, and Iran.
