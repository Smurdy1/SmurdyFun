Find the Country from a Point · North America · 23 countries

Identify the North America country that contains each point placed on the map.

What this North America quiz covers

North America in this quiz includes Canada, the United States, Mexico, Central America, and the sovereign Caribbean countries stored in the group. Three very large mainland countries share the map with many smaller tropical states.

How the point map quiz works

A point appears somewhere inside one of the places in this group. Type the name of the country that contains it. The round covers all 23 countries and tests whether you understand the full area of each place rather than only recognizing its outline.

Gameplay tip: Check the point against coastlines and nearby countries before answering, especially when it sits close to a border.

What you'll practice

This catches a different kind of mistake: knowing a place's general location but not how far its territory extends. Points near borders are especially good at exposing that gap.

A good way to start

Decide whether the point is near a coast, inland, or on an island. Then compare it with the nearest borders and recognizable shapes.

When this mode helps

Try this when you know the main outlines and want a more exact test of where one place ends and another begins.

One mistake to watch for

Don't pick the closest name you recognize. The answer is the place that actually contains the point.

What makes this group challenging

The Caribbean islands and the narrow Central American isthmus require closer map reading than Canada, the United States, or Mexico.

Study tip

Master the mainland from north to south, then learn the Caribbean as a separate cluster before combining them.
