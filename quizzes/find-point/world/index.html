Find the Country from a Point: World Quiz

Find the Country from a Point / World / 201 countries

Identify which country in this set contains each point placed on the map.

Points can land almost anywhere

The full world map brings together countries from every continent, from the largest continental states to small island nations. The round tests country names, locations, shapes, and regional context instead of concentrating on one part of the globe.

A point in Canada is one thing; a point near the edge of Rwanda, Belgium, or El Salvador is another.

Why World feels different

A continent quiz lets you keep one mental map loaded. World keeps making you throw that map away and load another one, which is why familiar countries can suddenly take a second longer.

How the point map quiz works

A point appears somewhere inside one of the places in this group. Type the name of the country that contains it. The round covers all 201 countries and tests whether you understand the full area of each place rather than only recognizing its outline.

Try this when you know the main outlines and want a more exact test of where one place ends and another begins.

Tip: Check the point against coastlines and nearby countries before answering, especially when it sits close to a border.

What you'll practice

This catches a different kind of mistake: knowing a place's general location but not how far its territory extends. Points near borders are especially good at exposing that gap.

Where World usually bites back

The hardest part is switching quickly between very different parts of the world. Small countries in Europe, the Caribbean, and the Pacific can also be easy to overlook.
