Find the Country from a Point / South America / 12 countries

Identify the South America country that contains each point placed on the map.

What this South America quiz covers

South America has a manageable number of countries and several clear coastal shapes. The group runs from Colombia and Venezuela in the north to Argentina and Chile in the south.

How the point map quiz works

A point appears somewhere inside one of the places in this group. Type the name of the country that contains it. The round covers all 12 countries and tests whether you understand the full area of each place rather than only recognizing its outline.

Gameplay tip: Check the point against coastlines and nearby countries before answering, especially when it sits close to a border.

What you'll practice

This catches a different kind of mistake: knowing a place's general location but not how far its territory extends. Points near borders are especially good at exposing that gap.

A good way to start

Decide whether the point is near a coast, inland, or on an island. Then compare it with the nearest borders and recognizable shapes.

When this mode helps

Try this when you know the main outlines and want a more exact test of where one place ends and another begins.

One mistake to watch for

Don't pick the closest name you recognize. The answer is the place that actually contains the point.

What makes this group challenging

The Guianas in the northeast and the inland positions of Bolivia and Paraguay are the places most likely to cause mistakes.

Study tip

Use Brazil as the central anchor, then learn which countries touch its long border and which do not.

Example countries

  • Brazil
  • Argentina
  • Colombia
  • Chile
  • Peru
Countries included in this quiz (12)

Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil, Chile, Colombia, Ecuador, Guyana, Paraguay, Peru, Suriname, Uruguay, Venezuela.
