Find the Country from a Point / European Union / 27 countries

Identify the European Union country that contains each point placed on the map.

What this European Union quiz covers

The European Union group contains the current EU member states as both a political and geographic set. It helps separate EU countries from nearby non-members such as Norway, Switzerland, the United Kingdom, and several Balkan states.

How the point map quiz works

A point appears somewhere inside one of the places in this group. Type the name of the country that contains it. The round covers all 27 countries and tests whether you understand the full area of each place rather than only recognizing its outline.

Gameplay tip: Check the point against coastlines and nearby countries before answering, especially when it sits close to a border.

What you'll practice

This catches a different kind of mistake: knowing a place's general location but not how far its territory extends. Points near borders are especially good at exposing that gap.

A good way to start

Decide whether the point is near a coast, inland, or on an island. Then compare it with the nearest borders and recognizable shapes.

When this mode helps

Try this when you know the main outlines and want a more exact test of where one place ends and another begins.

One mistake to watch for

Don't pick the closest name you recognize. The answer is the place that actually contains the point.

What makes this group challenging

The main difficulty is remembering membership, not only location. Some countries in central and southeastern Europe are geographically close to the EU but are not members.

Study tip

First learn the non-EU countries that sit inside or beside the wider European map, then the membership pattern becomes easier to see.
