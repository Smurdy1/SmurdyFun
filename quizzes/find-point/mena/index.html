What this Middle East and North Africa quiz covers

MENA, short for Middle East and North Africa, stretches from Morocco and Algeria to Iran and the Arabian Peninsula. The group crosses Africa and Asia while keeping together countries that are often studied as one region.

How the point map quiz works

A point appears somewhere inside one of the places in this group. Type the name of the country that contains it. The round covers all 21 countries and tests whether you understand the full area of each place rather than only recognizing its outline.

Gameplay tip: Check the point against coastlines and nearby countries before answering, especially when it sits close to a border.

What you'll practice

This catches a different kind of mistake: knowing a place's general location but not how far its territory extends. Points near borders are especially good at exposing that gap.

A good way to start

Decide whether the point is near a coast, inland, or on an island. Then compare it with the nearest borders and recognizable shapes.

When this mode helps

Try this when you know the main outlines and want a more exact test of where one place ends and another begins.

One mistake to watch for

Don't pick the closest name you recognize. The answer is the place that actually contains the point.

What makes this group challenging

The group crosses Africa and Asia, and the smaller countries near the eastern Mediterranean and Persian Gulf can be difficult at the same scale as Algeria or Saudi Arabia.

Study tip

Learn North Africa west to east, then attach the Levant, Arabian Peninsula, Iraq, and Iran.
