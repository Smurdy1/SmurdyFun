The Horn, the lakes, and the coast

East Africa in this group includes the Horn, the Great Lakes region, the Indian Ocean coast, and nearby island countries. Ethiopia, Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, and Somalia provide several recognizable anchors.

Ethiopia, Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, and Somalia give you several strong shapes, then the smaller neighbors test the gaps between them.

How the point map quiz works

A point appears somewhere inside one of the places in this group. Type the name of the country that contains it. The round covers all 17 countries and tests whether you understand the full area of each place rather than only recognizing its outline.

Try this when you know the main outlines and want a more exact test of where one place ends and another begins.

Tip: Check the point against coastlines and nearby countries before answering, especially when it sits close to a border.
