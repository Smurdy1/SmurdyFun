Find the Country from a Point / Former Soviet Union / 15 countries

Identify the Former Soviet Union country that contains each point placed on the map.

What this Former Soviet Union quiz covers

The Former Soviet Union group contains the countries that became independent after the Soviet Union dissolved. Its map stretches from the Baltic Sea and Eastern Europe through the Caucasus and Central Asia.

How the point map quiz works

A point appears somewhere inside one of the places in this group. Type the name of the country that contains it. The round covers all 15 countries and tests whether you understand the full area of each place rather than only recognizing its outline.

Gameplay tip: Check the point against coastlines and nearby countries before answering, especially when it sits close to a border.

What you'll practice

This catches a different kind of mistake: knowing a place's general location but not how far its territory extends. Points near borders are especially good at exposing that gap.

A good way to start

Decide whether the point is near a coast, inland, or on an island. Then compare it with the nearest borders and recognizable shapes.

When this mode helps

Try this when you know the main outlines and want a more exact test of where one place ends and another begins.

One mistake to watch for

Don't pick the closest name you recognize. The answer is the place that actually contains the point.

What makes this group challenging

The countries are spread across several subregions, and the five Central Asian states are often confused because of their similar names and inland positions.

Study tip

Learn the Baltic states, the eastern European states, the Caucasus, and Central Asia as separate clusters before combining them.
