Identify the Middle East country that contains each point placed on the map.

What this Middle East quiz covers

The Middle East group centers on the eastern Mediterranean, the Arabian Peninsula, Iran, Iraq, and nearby parts of western Asia. It gives players a focused alternative to practicing the whole Asian continent at once.

How the point map quiz works

A point appears somewhere inside one of the places in this group. Type the name of the country that contains it. The round covers all 17 countries and tests whether you understand the full area of each place rather than only recognizing its outline.

Gameplay tip: Check the point against coastlines and nearby countries before answering, especially when it sits close to a border.

What you'll practice

This catches a different kind of mistake: knowing a place's general location but not how far its territory extends. Points near borders are especially good at exposing that gap.

A good way to start

Decide whether the point is near a coast, inland, or on an island. Then compare it with the nearest borders and recognizable shapes.

When this mode helps

Try this when you know the main outlines and want a more exact test of where one place ends and another begins.

One mistake to watch for

Don't pick the closest name you recognize. The answer is the place that actually contains the point.

What makes this group challenging

The Levant and Persian Gulf contain several small neighboring countries, while the boundaries between regional definitions can vary between sources.

Study tip

Learn the Arabian Peninsula as one shape, then place the Levant, Iraq, and Iran around it.
