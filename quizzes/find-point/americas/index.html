Find the Country from a Point / Americas / 35 countries

Identify the Americas country that contains each point placed on the map.

What this Americas quiz covers

The Americas round combines North America, Central America, the Caribbean, and South America. It is a full western hemisphere challenge with both continental countries and small island nations.

How the point map quiz works

A point appears somewhere inside one of the places in this group. Type the name of the country that contains it. The round covers all 35 countries and tests whether you understand the full area of each place rather than only recognizing its outline.

Gameplay tip: Check the point against coastlines and nearby countries before answering, especially when it sits close to a border.

What you'll practice

This catches a different kind of mistake: knowing a place's general location but not how far its territory extends. Points near borders are especially good at exposing that gap.

A good way to start

Decide whether the point is near a coast, inland, or on an island. Then compare it with the nearest borders and recognizable shapes.

When this mode helps

Try this when you know the main outlines and want a more exact test of where one place ends and another begins.

One mistake to watch for

Don't pick the closest name you recognize. The answer is the place that actually contains the point.

What makes this group challenging

The map moves from very large countries to tiny islands, so players must adjust quickly between different scales and regional patterns.

Study tip

Master North America and South America separately, then use this quiz to connect them through Central America and the Caribbean.
