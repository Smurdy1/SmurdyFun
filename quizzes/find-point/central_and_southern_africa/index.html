Find the Country from a Point / Central and Southern Africa / 18 countries

Identify the Central and Southern Africa country that contains each point placed on the map.

What this Central and Southern Africa quiz covers

Central and Southern Africa covers the Congo Basin and the countries toward the continent's southern end. The region includes large inland states, two ocean coastlines, and several smaller landlocked countries.

How the point map quiz works

A point appears somewhere inside one of the places in this group. Type the name of the country that contains it. The round covers all 18 countries and tests whether you understand the full area of each place rather than only recognizing its outline.

Gameplay tip: Check the point against coastlines and nearby countries before answering, especially when it sits close to a border.

What you'll practice

This catches a different kind of mistake: knowing a place's general location but not how far its territory extends. Points near borders are especially good at exposing that gap.

A good way to start

Decide whether the point is near a coast, inland, or on an island. Then compare it with the nearest borders and recognizable shapes.

When this mode helps

Try this when you know the main outlines and want a more exact test of where one place ends and another begins.

One mistake to watch for

Don't pick the closest name you recognize. The answer is the place that actually contains the point.

What makes this group challenging

The two Congos are easy to confuse, and the compact countries around South Africa require closer attention than the larger states to the north.

Study tip

Learn the Congo Basin first, then move south through Angola, Zambia, Zimbabwe, Botswana, and the countries around South Africa.
