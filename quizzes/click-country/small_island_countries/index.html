What this Small Island Countries quiz covers

Small Island Countries brings together independent island states from the Caribbean, Atlantic, Indian Ocean, Mediterranean, and Pacific. The set is designed for places that often appear only as small shapes or points.

How the click map quiz works

The quiz gives you one country at a time. Click the matching area on the map, then continue until you have completed all 27 countries. Borders remain visible, so this mode works well for first learning the shapes and positions.

Gameplay tip: Start with the largest or most familiar places, then use their borders to place nearby answers.

What you'll practice

After a few rounds, you should start recognizing outlines faster and remembering which places border each other. Keeping the name and shape together also makes the location easier to recall later.

A good way to start

Don't scan the whole map at random. Find the right part of the map first, pick out one or two places you know, and work from their borders.

When this mode helps

Start here if the Small Island Countries map is new to you, if you haven't practiced it in a while, or if you want to get faster without hiding the borders yet.

One mistake to watch for

Once you recognize the general area, it's easy to click a familiar neighbor too quickly. Check the target's borders before choosing.

What makes this group challenging

Many answers are far apart and visually small, so players cannot rely on a chain of neighboring land borders.

Study tip

Study the islands by ocean or subregion instead of trying to memorize the full list in one pass.
