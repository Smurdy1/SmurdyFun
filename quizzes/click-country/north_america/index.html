Click the Countries / North America / 23 countries

Learn the North America map by clicking each country when its name appears.

What this North America quiz covers

North America in this quiz includes Canada, the United States, Mexico, Central America, and the sovereign Caribbean countries stored in the group. Three very large mainland countries share the map with many smaller tropical states.

How the click map quiz works

The quiz gives you one country at a time. Click the matching area on the map, then continue until you have completed all 23 countries. Borders remain visible, so this mode works well for first learning the shapes and positions.

Start here if the North America map is new to you, if you haven't practiced it in a while, or if you want to get faster without hiding the borders yet.

Tip: Start with the largest or most familiar places, then use their borders to place nearby answers.
