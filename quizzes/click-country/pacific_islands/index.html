Click the Countries: Pacific Islands Map Quiz

Click the Countries / Pacific Islands / 12 countries

Learn the Pacific Islands map by clicking each country when its name appears.

A quiz spread across half an ocean

Pacific Islands focuses on the independent countries scattered across the Pacific, including Melanesian, Micronesian, and Polynesian states in Smurdy's group list. Australia and New Zealand are left out so the island nations remain the focus.

Fiji, Kiribati, the Marshall Islands, Samoa, and Palau are separated by distances that would span continents elsewhere.

Distance is misleading

On a world map the islands can look like a loose spray of dots. Grouping them into Micronesia, Melanesia, and Polynesia gives the empty ocean some structure.

How the click map quiz works

The quiz gives you one country at a time. Click the matching area on the map, then continue until you have completed all 12 countries. Borders remain visible, so this mode works well for first learning the shapes and positions.

Start here if the Pacific Islands map is new to you, if you haven't practiced it in a while, or if you want to get faster without hiding the borders yet.

Tip: Start with the largest or most familiar places, then use their borders to place nearby answers.

The map gives you very few landmarks

The countries are separated by huge ocean distances, and several are represented by very small map areas. Relative position is more useful than border shape.
