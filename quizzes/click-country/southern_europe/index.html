Click the Countries / Southern Europe / 18 countries

Learn the Southern Europe map by clicking each country when its name appears.

What this Southern Europe quiz covers

Southern Europe centers on the Mediterranean-facing countries of the Iberian Peninsula, Italy, the Balkans, and nearby microstates included in the group. It gives concentrated practice for the lower half of the European map.

How the click map quiz works

The quiz gives you one country at a time. Click the matching area on the map, then continue until you have completed all 18 countries. Borders remain visible, so this mode works well for first learning the shapes and positions.

Gameplay tip: Start with the largest or most familiar places, then use their borders to place nearby answers.

What you'll practice

After a few rounds, you should start recognizing outlines faster and remembering which places border each other. Keeping the name and shape together also makes the location easier to recall later.

A good way to start

Don't scan the whole map at random. Find the right part of the map first, pick out one or two places you know, and work from their borders.

When this mode helps

Start here if the Southern Europe map is new to you, if you haven't practiced it in a while, or if you want to get faster without hiding the borders yet.

One mistake to watch for

Once you recognize the general area, it's easy to click a familiar neighbor too quickly. Check the target's borders before choosing.

What makes this group challenging

The region combines familiar peninsulas with several very small countries and tightly packed Balkan borders.

Study tip

Learn the three major peninsulas first, then add the island countries and microstates around them.
