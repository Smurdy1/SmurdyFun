Click the Countries: Southern Europe Map Quiz

Click the Countries / Southern Europe / 18 countries

Learn the Southern Europe map by clicking each country when its name appears.

Peninsulas, islands, and microstates

Southern Europe centers on the Mediterranean-facing countries of the Iberian Peninsula, Italy, the Balkans, and nearby microstates included in the group. It gives concentrated practice for the lower half of the European map.

Spain, Italy, and Greece are excellent anchors, but Malta, San Marino, Vatican City, and the Balkans make the small-scale details matter.

How the click map quiz works

The quiz gives you one country at a time. Click the matching area on the map, then continue until you have completed all 18 countries. Borders remain visible, so this mode works well for first learning the shapes and positions.

Start here if the Southern Europe map is new to you, if you haven't practiced it in a while, or if you want to get faster without hiding the borders yet.

Tip: Start with the largest or most familiar places, then use their borders to place nearby answers.
