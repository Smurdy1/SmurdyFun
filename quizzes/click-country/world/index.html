Click the Countries / World / 201 countries

Learn the World map by clicking each country when its name appears.

What this World quiz covers

The full world map brings together countries from every continent, from the largest continental states to small island nations. The round tests country names, locations, shapes, and regional context instead of concentrating on one part of the globe.

How the click map quiz works

The quiz gives you one country at a time. Click the matching area on the map, then continue until you have completed all 201 countries. Borders remain visible, so this mode works well for first learning the shapes and positions.

Gameplay tip: Start with the largest or most familiar places, then use their borders to place nearby answers.

What you'll practice

After a few rounds, you should start recognizing outlines faster and remembering which places border each other. Keeping the name and shape together also makes the location easier to recall later.

A good way to start

Don't scan the whole map at random. Find the right part of the map first, pick out one or two places you know, and work from their borders.

When this mode helps

Start here if the World map is new to you, if you haven't practiced it in a while, or if you want to get faster without hiding the borders yet.

One mistake to watch for

Once you recognize the general area, it's easy to click a familiar neighbor too quickly. Check the target's borders before choosing.

What makes this group challenging

The hardest part is switching quickly between very different parts of the world. Small countries in Europe, the Caribbean, and the Pacific can also be easy to overlook.

Study tip

Learn one continent at a time, then return to the world quiz to practice changing regions without warning.
