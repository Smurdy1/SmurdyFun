Click the Countries: World Map Quiz

Click the Countries / World / 201 countries

Learn the World map by clicking each country when its name appears.

The full world map brings together countries from every continent, from the largest continental states to small island nations. The round tests country names, locations, shapes, and regional context instead of concentrating on one part of the globe.

One round can jump from Argentina to Kyrgyzstan to Fiji with no warning.

Why World feels different

A continent quiz lets you keep one mental map loaded. World keeps making you throw that map away and load another one, which is why familiar countries can suddenly take a second longer.

How the click map quiz works

The quiz gives you one country at a time. Click the matching area on the map, then continue until you have completed all 201 countries. Borders remain visible, so this mode works well for first learning the shapes and positions.

Tip: Start with the largest or most familiar places, then use their borders to place nearby answers.

Build it continent by continent

Learn one continent at a time, then return to the world quiz to practice changing regions without warning.
