Learn the Caribbean Islands map by clicking each country when its name appears.

What this Caribbean Islands quiz covers

The Caribbean group focuses on sovereign countries spread across the Caribbean Sea and nearby Atlantic waters. It includes the large islands of the Greater Antilles and the smaller independent states curving toward South America.

How the click map quiz works

The quiz gives you one country at a time. Click the matching area on the map, then continue until you have completed all 13 countries. Borders remain visible, so this mode works well for first learning the shapes and positions.

Start here if the Caribbean Islands map is new to you, if you haven't practiced it in a while, or if you want to get faster without hiding the borders yet.

Tip: Start with the largest or most familiar places, then use their borders to place nearby answers.

What makes this group challenging

Several countries occupy small islands that appear close together, and some neighboring islands are territories rather than independent countries.
