Click the Countries: Tiny Countries Map Quiz

Click the Countries / Tiny Countries / 28 countries

Learn the Tiny Countries map by clicking each country when its name appears.

The map's smallest targets

Tiny Countries gathers small sovereign states from Europe, the Caribbean, the Indian Ocean, and the Pacific. These are the places most likely to disappear into a normal world map at first glance.

Monaco, Liechtenstein, San Marino, Vatican City, Nauru, and Tuvalu turn ordinary map knowledge into a precision exercise.

Zoom is part of the challenge

This set is intentionally awkward at normal world-map scale. Knowing the correct neighborhood first matters more than trying to spot a microscopic shape from far away.

How the click map quiz works

The quiz gives you one country at a time. Click the matching area on the map, then continue until you have completed all 28 countries. Borders remain visible, so this mode works well for first learning the shapes and positions.

Start here if the Tiny Countries map is new to you, if you haven't practiced it in a while, or if you want to get faster without hiding the borders yet.

Tip: Start with the largest or most familiar places, then use their borders to place nearby answers.

Precision matters more than recognition

Most answers occupy very little screen space, and many do not share land borders with larger countries. Precise zooming and recognition of regional context matter more than outline shape.
