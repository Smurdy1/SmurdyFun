Learn the Africa map by clicking each country when its name appears.

What this Africa quiz covers

Africa contains more than fifty answers in this group, including North Africa, West Africa, the Horn, central Africa, southern Africa, and nearby island countries. It is one of the site's most detailed continent quizzes.

How the click map quiz works

The quiz gives you one country at a time. Click the matching area on the map, then continue until you have completed all 56 countries. Borders remain visible, so this mode works well for first learning the shapes and positions.

Gameplay tip: Start with the largest or most familiar places, then use their borders to place nearby answers.

What you'll practice

After a few rounds, you should start recognizing outlines faster and remembering which places border each other. Keeping the name and shape together also makes the location easier to recall later.

A good way to start

Don't scan the whole map at random. Find the right part of the map first, pick out one or two places you know, and work from their borders.

When this mode helps

Start here if the Africa map is new to you, if you haven't practiced it in a while, or if you want to get faster without hiding the borders yet.

One mistake to watch for

Once you recognize the general area, it's easy to click a familiar neighbor too quickly. Check the target's borders before choosing.

What makes this group challenging

West Africa contains many smaller countries with short coastlines, while central and eastern Africa have several large inland neighbors that can blend together at first.

Study tip

Learn the coastline first, then use those coastal countries to place the landlocked countries behind them.
