Click the Countries: Latin America Map Quiz

Click the Countries / Latin America / 21 countries

Learn the Latin America map by clicking each country when its name appears.

Several map patterns under one label

Latin America connects Mexico, Central America, much of the Caribbean, and South America in one broad regional set. The grouping mainly follows shared Spanish, Portuguese, and French colonial language history.

Mexico, Cuba, Brazil, and Chile all belong here, so the quiz moves between mainland chains, islands, and an entire continent.

How the click map quiz works

The quiz gives you one country at a time. Click the matching area on the map, then continue until you have completed all 21 countries. Borders remain visible, so this mode works well for first learning the shapes and positions.

Start here if the Latin America map is new to you, if you haven't practiced it in a while, or if you want to get faster without hiding the borders yet.

Tip: Start with the largest or most familiar places, then use their borders to place nearby answers.

Break it into three chunks

Learn Central America and South America separately, then add the Caribbean countries included in the group.
