Click the Countries / Oceania / 14 countries

Learn the Oceania map by clicking each country when its name appears.

What this Oceania quiz covers

Oceania covers Australia, New Zealand, Papua New Guinea, and the independent island countries of the Pacific. Unlike a continuous continental map, this group is built around widely separated land areas.

How the click map quiz works

The quiz gives you one country at a time. Click the matching area on the map, then continue until you have completed all 14 countries. Borders remain visible, so this mode works well for first learning the shapes and positions.

Gameplay tip: Start with the largest or most familiar places, then use their borders to place nearby answers.

What you'll practice

After a few rounds, you should start recognizing outlines faster and remembering which places border each other. Keeping the name and shape together also makes the location easier to recall later.

A good way to start

Don't scan the whole map at random. Find the right part of the map first, pick out one or two places you know, and work from their borders.

When this mode helps

Start here if the Oceania map is new to you, if you haven't practiced it in a while, or if you want to get faster without hiding the borders yet.

One mistake to watch for

Once you recognize the general area, it's easy to click a familiar neighbor too quickly. Check the target's borders before choosing.

What makes this group challenging

Many countries are small and far apart, so relative position matters more than shared land borders. Several island nations can look like isolated points at a world scale.

Study tip

Use Australia, New Zealand, and Papua New Guinea as anchors, then learn the island countries by their position north, east, or northeast of those anchors.
