Spanish-Speaking Countries Map Quiz – Click the Countries

Click the Countries / Spanish-Speaking Countries / 20 countries

Learn this set by clicking each country when its name appears.

What this quiz covers

This set covers the 20 sovereign countries where Spanish is a national official or dominant language: 18 in the Americas, plus Spain in Europe and Equatorial Guinea in Africa. It focuses on countries rather than every territory or place where Spanish is widely spoken.

How the click map quiz works

The quiz gives you one country at a time. Click the matching area on the map, then continue until you have completed all 20 countries. Borders remain visible, so this mode works well for first learning the shapes and positions.

Gameplay tip: Start with the largest or most familiar places, then use their borders to place nearby answers.

What you'll practice

After a few rounds, you should start recognizing outlines faster and remembering which places border each other. Keeping the name and shape together also makes the location easier to recall later.

A good way to start

Don't scan the whole map at random. Find the right part of the map first, pick out one or two places you know, and work from their borders.

When this mode helps

Start here if this map is new to you, if you have not practiced it in a while, or if you want to get faster before hiding the borders.

One mistake to watch for

Once you recognize the general area, it's easy to click a familiar neighbor too quickly. Check the target's borders before choosing.

What makes this set challenging

The group crosses three continents, and several nearby countries are intentionally excluded because Spanish is not their main national language. Central America and northern South America can also be easy to mix up when the full set is shuffled.

Study tip

Learn the set in clusters: Mexico and Central America, the Spanish-speaking Caribbean, South America, then Spain and Equatorial Guinea. After each cluster feels easy, mix all 20 together.
