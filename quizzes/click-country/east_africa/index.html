The Horn, the lakes, and the coast

East Africa in this group includes the Horn, the Great Lakes region, the Indian Ocean coast, and nearby island countries. Ethiopia, Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, and Somalia provide several recognizable anchors.

Ethiopia, Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, and Somalia give you several strong shapes, then the smaller neighbors test the gaps between them.

How the click map quiz works

The quiz gives you one country at a time. Click the matching area on the map, then continue until you have completed all 17 countries. Borders remain visible, so this mode works well for first learning the shapes and positions.

Start here if the East Africa map is new to you, if you haven't practiced it in a while, or if you want to get faster without hiding the borders yet.

Tip: Start with the largest or most familiar places, then use their borders to place nearby answers.
