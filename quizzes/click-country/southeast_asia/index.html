Click the Countries / Southeast Asia / 11 countries

Learn the Southeast Asia map by clicking each country when its name appears.

What this Southeast Asia quiz covers

Southeast Asia includes the mainland countries between India and China along with the major island states to the south and east. Thailand, Vietnam, Indonesia, the Philippines, and Malaysia are among the best-known landmarks in the group.

How the click map quiz works

The quiz gives you one country at a time. Click the matching area on the map, then continue until you have completed all 11 countries. Borders remain visible, so this mode works well for first learning the shapes and positions.

Gameplay tip: Start with the largest or most familiar places, then use their borders to place nearby answers.

What you'll practice

After a few rounds, you should start recognizing outlines faster and remembering which places border each other. Keeping the name and shape together also makes the location easier to recall later.

A good way to start

Don't scan the whole map at random. Find the right part of the map first, pick out one or two places you know, and work from their borders.

When this mode helps

Start here if the Southeast Asia map is new to you, if you haven't practiced it in a while, or if you want to get faster without hiding the borders yet.

One mistake to watch for

Once you recognize the general area, it's easy to click a familiar neighbor too quickly. Check the target's borders before choosing.

What makes this group challenging

Mainland borders are tightly packed, while Indonesia and the Philippines are spread across many islands. Malaysia is also split between the Malay Peninsula and Borneo.

Study tip

Study mainland Southeast Asia from west to east, then learn the island countries as a second map pattern.
