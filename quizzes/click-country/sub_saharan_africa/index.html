What this Sub-Saharan Africa quiz covers

Sub-Saharan Africa contains the countries south of the Sahara according to Smurdy's stored group list. Removing North Africa still leaves a large map spanning West, Central, East, and Southern Africa.

How the click map quiz works

The quiz gives you one country at a time. Click the matching area on the map, then continue until you have completed all 49 countries. Borders remain visible, so this mode works well for first learning the shapes and positions.

Gameplay tip: Start with the largest or most familiar places, then use their borders to place nearby answers.

What you'll practice

After a few rounds, you should start recognizing outlines faster and remembering which places border each other. Keeping the name and shape together also makes the location easier to recall later.

A good way to start

Don't scan the whole map at random. Find the right part of the map first, pick out one or two places you know, and work from their borders.

When this mode helps

Start here if the Sub-Saharan Africa map is new to you, if you haven't practiced it in a while, or if you want to get faster without hiding the borders yet.

One mistake to watch for

Once you recognize the general area, it's easy to click a familiar neighbor too quickly. Check the target's borders before choosing.

What makes this group challenging

The group is still large and includes many landlocked countries, so memorizing regional clusters is more effective than treating it as one uninterrupted list.

Study tip

Practice West, East, Central, and Southern Africa separately before using this combined quiz.
