Click the Countries: Eastern Europe Map Quiz

Click the Countries / Eastern Europe / 10 countries

Learn the Eastern Europe map by clicking each country when its name appears.

A compact region with few easy coastlines

Eastern Europe provides a smaller practice set for the countries on the eastern side of the European map. The exact regional boundary varies by source, so Smurdy follows the consistent country list stored in its group file.

Belarus, Ukraine, Moldova, Romania, and Bulgaria are easier when you remember who touches whom than when you search for one distinctive outline.

How the click map quiz works

The quiz gives you one country at a time. Click the matching area on the map, then continue until you have completed all 10 countries. Borders remain visible, so this mode works well for first learning the shapes and positions.

Start here if the Eastern Europe map is new to you, if you haven't practiced it in a while, or if you want to get faster without hiding the borders yet.

Tip: Start with the largest or most familiar places, then use their borders to place nearby answers.
