South America Map Quiz: Click All 12 Countries

Click the Countries / South America / 12 countries

Learn the 12 sovereign countries of South America by clicking their visible map outlines. The compact set is ideal for practicing country shapes, shared borders, coastlines, and regional order.

Countries included in the South America click quiz

This quiz covers all 12 sovereign South American countries. The map runs from Colombia, Venezuela, Guyana, and Suriname in the north through Brazil and the inland states to Chile, Argentina, and Uruguay in the south.

Use Brazil to organize the continent

Brazil borders every sovereign South American country except Chile and Ecuador. That makes one question especially useful: does the target touch Brazil? Combining that fact with the Pacific and Atlantic coastlines quickly narrows most answers.

How the click map quiz works

Read the country name, find its bordered shape on the South America map, and click it. Continue through all 12 answers, using coastlines and neighboring countries whenever a shape is unfamiliar.

Gameplay tip: Locate Brazil first as the continent's largest anchor, then compare the target with the countries around its border.

What you'll practice

After a few rounds, you should start recognizing outlines faster and remembering which places border each other. Keeping the name and shape together also makes the location easier to recall later.

A good way to start

Don't scan the whole map at random. Find the right part of the map first, pick out one or two places you know, and work from their borders.

When this mode helps

Start here if the South America map is new to you, if you haven't practiced it in a while, or if you want to get faster without hiding the borders yet.

One mistake to watch for

Once you recognize the general area, it's easy to click a familiar neighbor too quickly. Check the target's borders before choosing.

South American countries commonly mixed up

Guyana and Suriname are small neighbors on the northeastern coast, while Uruguay and Paraguay are sometimes confused because both sit near southern Brazil. Bolivia's inland position can also be mistaken for neighboring Peru or Paraguay.

The fastest way to learn the South America map

Memorize the Pacific coast from Colombia to Chile, then learn the northern Atlantic countries and the Southern Cone. Add Bolivia and Paraguay last as the two landlocked countries, always relating them back to Brazil.
