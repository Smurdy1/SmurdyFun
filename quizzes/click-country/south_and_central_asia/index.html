Mountains and landlocked neighbors

South and Central Asia links the Indian subcontinent with the landlocked countries between the Caspian Sea, China, Russia, and Afghanistan. The combined set shows how the two neighboring regions fit together.

Kazakhstan and India are strong anchors, but Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan, Nepal, Bhutan, and Bangladesh compress a lot of geography into narrow spaces.

How the click map quiz works

The quiz gives you one country at a time. Click the matching area on the map, then continue until you have completed all 13 countries. Borders remain visible, so this mode works well for first learning the shapes and positions.

Start here if the South and Central Asia map is new to you, if you haven't practiced it in a while, or if you want to get faster without hiding the borders yet.

Tip: Start with the largest or most familiar places, then use their borders to place nearby answers.

Build a west-to-east sequence
