Learn the Americas map by clicking each country when its name appears.

What this Americas quiz covers

The Americas round combines North America, Central America, the Caribbean, and South America. It is a full western hemisphere challenge with both continental countries and small island nations.

How the click map quiz works

The quiz gives you one country at a time. Click the matching area on the map, then continue until you have completed all 35 countries. Borders remain visible, so this mode works well for first learning the shapes and positions.

Gameplay tip: Start with the largest or most familiar places, then use their borders to place nearby answers.

What you'll practice

After a few rounds, you should start recognizing outlines faster and remembering which places border each other. Keeping the name and shape together also makes the location easier to recall later.

A good way to start

Don't scan the whole map at random. Find the right part of the map first, pick out one or two places you know, and work from their borders.

When this mode helps

Start here if the Americas map is new to you, if you haven't practiced it in a while, or if you want to get faster without hiding the borders yet.

One mistake to watch for

Once you recognize the general area, it's easy to click a familiar neighbor too quickly. Check the target's borders before choosing.

What makes this group challenging

The map moves from very large countries to tiny islands, so players must adjust quickly between different scales and regional patterns.

Study tip

Master North America and South America separately, then use this quiz to connect them through Central America and the Caribbean.
