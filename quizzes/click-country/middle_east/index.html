Click the Countries / Middle East / 17 countries

Learn the Middle East map by clicking each country when its name appears.

What this Middle East quiz covers

The Middle East group centers on the eastern Mediterranean, the Arabian Peninsula, Iran, Iraq, and nearby parts of western Asia. It gives players a focused alternative to practicing the whole Asian continent at once.

How the click map quiz works

The quiz gives you one country at a time. Click the matching area on the map, then continue until you have completed all 17 countries. Borders remain visible, so this mode works well for first learning the shapes and positions.

Gameplay tip: Start with the largest or most familiar places, then use their borders to place nearby answers.

What you'll practice

After a few rounds, you should start recognizing outlines faster and remembering which places border each other. Keeping the name and shape together also makes the location easier to recall later.

A good way to start

Don't scan the whole map at random. Find the right part of the map first, pick out one or two places you know, and work from their borders.

When this mode helps

Start here if the Middle East map is new to you, if you haven't practiced it in a while, or if you want to get faster without hiding the borders yet.

One mistake to watch for

Once you recognize the general area, it's easy to click a familiar neighbor too quickly. Check the target's borders before choosing.

What makes this group challenging

The Levant and Persian Gulf contain several small neighboring countries, while the boundaries between regional definitions can vary between sources.

Study tip

Learn the Arabian Peninsula as one shape, then place the Levant, Iraq, and Iran around it.
