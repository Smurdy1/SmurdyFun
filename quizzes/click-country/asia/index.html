Click the Countries: Asia Map Quiz

Click the Countries / Asia / 51 countries

Learn the Asia map by clicking each country when its name appears.

Long distances, sharp regional shifts

Asia stretches from the Middle East and Central Asia to India, China, Southeast Asia, and the Pacific coast. The map combines some of the world's largest countries with dense clusters of smaller neighbors.

Japan, Uzbekistan, Sri Lanka, and Saudi Arabia all belong to the same quiz, but almost none of the same visual shortcuts carry between them.

How the click map quiz works

The quiz gives you one country at a time. Click the matching area on the map, then continue until you have completed all 51 countries. Borders remain visible, so this mode works well for first learning the shapes and positions.

Start here if the Asia map is new to you, if you haven't practiced it in a while, or if you want to get faster without hiding the borders yet.

Tip: Start with the largest or most familiar places, then use their borders to place nearby answers.

Central and western Asia slow things down

Central Asia, the Caucasus, and mainland Southeast Asia contain several neighboring countries that are commonly confused. The continent also stretches across a very wide map.
