Type the Countries: Central and Southern Africa Map Quiz

Type the Countries / Central and Southern Africa / 18 countries

Practice the names and locations of Central and Southern Africa by typing the highlighted countries.

Big interiors and a crowded south

Central and Southern Africa covers the Congo Basin and the countries toward the continent's southern end. The region includes large inland states, two ocean coastlines, and several smaller landlocked countries.

The Democratic Republic of the Congo, Zambia, Botswana, Lesotho, and Eswatini create very different scale problems in the same part of the map.

How the typing map quiz works

One country is highlighted on the map. Type its name to answer, using the map shape and location as your clue. The round continues through all 18 countries, making this useful for spelling practice as well as map recognition.

Try this once the country shapes in this set look familiar but some names still take a second to remember.

Tip: Say each answer in your head before typing it. That small pause helps connect the written name to the map.
