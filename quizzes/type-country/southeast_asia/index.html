Practice the names and locations of Southeast Asia by typing the highlighted countries.

What this Southeast Asia quiz covers

Southeast Asia includes the mainland countries between India and China along with the major island states to the south and east. Thailand, Vietnam, Indonesia, the Philippines, and Malaysia are among the best-known landmarks in the group.

How the typing map quiz works

One country is highlighted on the map. Type its name to answer, using the map shape and location as your clue. The round continues through all 11 countries, making this useful for spelling practice as well as map recognition.

Gameplay tip: Say each answer in your head before typing it. That small pause helps connect the written name to the map.

What you'll practice

Knowing roughly where a place is won't be enough here. You have to pull the full name from memory and spell it, which helps when your map recognition is ahead of your name recall.

A good way to start

First work out the subregion from the highlighted shape. Think of nearby places, say the answer in your head, and then type it.

When this mode helps

Try this once the shapes in Southeast Asia look familiar but some names still take a second to remember.

One mistake to watch for

Don't guess from the first letter or a vaguely similar outline. Check the highlighted area's position and neighbors first.

What makes this group challenging

Mainland borders are tightly packed, while Indonesia and the Philippines are spread across many islands. Malaysia is also split between the Malay Peninsula and Borneo.

Study tip

Study mainland Southeast Asia from west to east, then learn the island countries as a second map pattern.
