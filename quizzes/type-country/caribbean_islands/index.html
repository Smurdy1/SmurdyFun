Type the Countries: Caribbean Islands Map Quiz

Type the Countries / Caribbean Islands / 13 countries

Practice the names and locations of the Caribbean islands by typing the highlighted countries.

Tiny targets across a wide sea

The Caribbean group focuses on sovereign countries spread across the Caribbean Sea and nearby Atlantic waters. It includes the large islands of the Greater Antilles and the smaller independent states curving toward South America.

Jamaica and Cuba are forgiving; Dominica, Saint Lucia, and the smaller eastern islands are much less so.

Island order matters

For the Lesser Antilles, memorizing a north-to-south sequence is often more useful than trying to recognize every island shape at world-map scale.

How the typing map quiz works

One country is highlighted on the map. Type its name to answer, using the map shape and location as your clue. The round continues through all 13 countries, making this useful for spelling practice as well as map recognition.

Try this once the country shapes in this set look familiar but some names still take a second to remember.

Tip: Say each answer in your head before typing it. That small pause helps connect the written name to the map.

The eastern chain is the real test

Several countries occupy small islands that appear close together, and some neighboring islands are territories rather than independent countries.
