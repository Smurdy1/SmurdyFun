Type the Countries: North America Map Quiz

Type the Countries / North America / 23 countries

Practice the names and locations of North America by typing the highlighted countries.

From Canada to the Caribbean

North America in this quiz includes Canada, the United States, Mexico, Central America, and the sovereign Caribbean countries stored in the group. Three very large mainland countries share the map with many smaller tropical states.

Canada and Mexico dominate the land area, but Central America and the island countries create most of the close calls.

How the typing map quiz works

One country is highlighted on the map. Type its name to answer, using the map shape and location as your clue. The round continues through all 23 countries, making this useful for spelling practice as well as map recognition.

Try this once the country shapes in this set look familiar but some names still take a second to remember.

Tip: Say each answer in your head before typing it. That small pause helps connect the written name to the map.
