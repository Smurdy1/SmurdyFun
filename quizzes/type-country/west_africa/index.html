Practice the names and locations of West Africa by typing the highlighted countries.

What this West Africa quiz covers

West Africa runs from the Atlantic coast into the Sahel and the inland countries behind the Gulf of Guinea. The group includes Senegal, Mali, Ghana, Nigeria, Ivory Coast, and many smaller neighbors.

How the typing map quiz works

One country is highlighted on the map. Type its name to answer, using the map shape and location as your clue. The round continues through all 16 countries, making this useful for spelling practice as well as map recognition.

Gameplay tip: Say each answer in your head before typing it. That small pause helps connect the written name to the map.

What you'll practice

Knowing roughly where a place is won't be enough here. You have to pull the full name from memory and spell it, which helps when your map recognition is ahead of your name recall.

A good way to start

First work out the subregion from the highlighted shape. Think of nearby places, say the answer in your head, and then type it.

When this mode helps

Try this once the shapes in West Africa look familiar but some names still take a second to remember.

One mistake to watch for

Don't guess from the first letter or a vaguely similar outline. Check the highlighted area's position and neighbors first.

What makes this group challenging

The coastline contains many smaller countries packed between larger neighbors, while several inland states have similar broad shapes.

Study tip

Trace the coast from Senegal to Nigeria, then use that coastal order to place Mali, Burkina Faso, and Niger inland.
