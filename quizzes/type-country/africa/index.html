What this Africa quiz covers

Africa contains more than fifty answers in this group, including North Africa, West Africa, the Horn, central Africa, southern Africa, and nearby island countries. It is one of the site's most detailed continent quizzes.

How the typing map quiz works

One country is highlighted on the map. Type its name to answer, using the map shape and location as your clue. The round continues through all 56 countries, making this useful for spelling practice as well as map recognition.

Gameplay tip: Say each answer in your head before typing it. That small pause helps connect the written name to the map.

What you'll practice

Knowing roughly where a place is won't be enough here. You have to pull the full name from memory and spell it, which helps when your map recognition is ahead of your name recall.

A good way to start

First work out the subregion from the highlighted shape. Think of nearby places, say the answer in your head, and then type it.

When this mode helps

Try this once the shapes in Africa look familiar but some names still take a second to remember.

One mistake to watch for

Don't guess from the first letter or a vaguely similar outline. Check the highlighted area's position and neighbors first.

What makes this group challenging

West Africa contains many smaller countries with short coastlines, while central and eastern Africa have several large inland neighbors that can blend together at first.

Study tip

Learn the coastline first, then use those coastal countries to place the landlocked countries behind them.
