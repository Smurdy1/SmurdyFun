Type the Countries / Small Island Countries / 27 countries

Practice the names and locations of small island countries by typing the highlighted countries.

The countries most likely to disappear at world zoom

Small Island Countries brings together independent island states from the Caribbean, Atlantic, Indian Ocean, Mediterranean, and Pacific. The set is designed for places that often appear only as small shapes or points.

The Bahamas, Mauritius, Malta, and the Maldives are memorable names, but their map targets are tiny and scattered far apart.

How the typing map quiz works

One country is highlighted on the map. Type its name to answer, using the map shape and location as your clue. The round continues through all 27 countries, making this useful for spelling practice as well as map recognition.

Try this once the country shapes in this set look familiar but some names still take a second to remember.

Tip: Say each answer in your head before typing it. That small pause helps connect the written name to the map.

Pair every island with a nearby anchor

Study the islands by ocean or subregion instead of trying to memorize the full list in one pass.
