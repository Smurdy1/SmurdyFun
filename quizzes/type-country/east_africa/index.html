Practice the names and locations of East Africa by typing the highlighted countries.

What this East Africa quiz covers

East Africa in this group includes the Horn, the Great Lakes region, the Indian Ocean coast, and nearby island countries. Ethiopia, Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, and Somalia provide several recognizable anchors.

How the typing map quiz works

One country is highlighted on the map. Type its name to answer, using the map shape and location as your clue. The round continues through all 17 countries, making this useful for spelling practice as well as map recognition.

Gameplay tip: Say each answer in your head before typing it. That small pause helps connect the written name to the map.

What you'll practice

Knowing roughly where a place is won't be enough here. You have to pull the full name from memory and spell it, which helps when your map recognition is ahead of your name recall.

A good way to start

First work out the subregion from the highlighted shape. Think of nearby places, say the answer in your head, and then type it.

When this mode helps

Try this once the shapes in East Africa look familiar but some names still take a second to remember.

One mistake to watch for

Don't guess from the first letter or a vaguely similar outline. Check the highlighted area's position and neighbors first.

What makes this group challenging

The Horn has distinctive coastlines, but the inland Great Lakes countries are smaller and closely grouped. Regional definitions can also differ near central and southern Africa.

Study tip

Begin with the Horn and Indian Ocean coastline, then work inland around Lake Victoria and the Great Lakes.
