What this Americas quiz covers

The Americas round combines North America, Central America, the Caribbean, and South America. It is a full western hemisphere challenge with both continental countries and small island nations.

How the typing map quiz works

One country is highlighted on the map. Type its name to answer, using the map shape and location as your clue. The round continues through all 35 countries, making this useful for spelling practice as well as map recognition.

Gameplay tip: Say each answer in your head before typing it. That small pause helps connect the written name to the map.

What you'll practice

Knowing roughly where a place is won't be enough here. You have to pull the full name from memory and spell it, which helps when your map recognition is ahead of your name recall.

A good way to start

First work out the subregion from the highlighted shape. Think of nearby places, say the answer in your head, and then type it.

When this mode helps

Try this once the shapes in Americas look familiar but some names still take a second to remember.

One mistake to watch for

Don't guess from the first letter or a vaguely similar outline. Check the highlighted area's position and neighbors first.

What makes this group challenging

The map moves from very large countries to tiny islands, so players must adjust quickly between different scales and regional patterns.

Study tip

Master North America and South America separately, then use this quiz to connect them through Central America and the Caribbean.
