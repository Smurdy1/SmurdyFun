Type the Countries: South and Central Asia Map Quiz

Type the Countries / South and Central Asia / 13 countries

Practice the names and locations of South and Central Asia by typing the highlighted countries.

Mountains and landlocked neighbors

South and Central Asia links the Indian subcontinent with the landlocked countries between the Caspian Sea, China, Russia, and Afghanistan. The combined set shows how the two neighboring regions fit together.

Kazakhstan and India are strong anchors, but Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan, Nepal, Bhutan, and Bangladesh compress a lot of geography into narrow spaces.

How the typing map quiz works

One country is highlighted on the map. Type its name to answer, using the map shape and location as your clue. The round continues through all 13 countries, making this useful for spelling practice as well as map recognition.

Try this once the country shapes in this set look familiar but some names still take a second to remember.

Tip: Say each answer in your head before typing it. That small pause helps connect the written name to the map.

Build a west-to-east sequence

Use India and Kazakhstan as the two main anchors, then learn the countries surrounding each one.
