What this Northern and Western Europe quiz covers

Northern and Western Europe brings together the Atlantic-facing countries, the British Isles, Scandinavia, the Nordic area, and much of western continental Europe. The map mixes long northern coastlines with compact central borders.

How the typing map quiz works

One country is highlighted on the map. Type its name to answer, using the map shape and location as your clue. The round continues through all 19 countries, making this useful for spelling practice as well as map recognition.

Try this once the country shapes in this set look familiar but some names still take a second to remember.

Tip: Say each answer in your head before typing it. That small pause helps connect the written name to the map.
