Type the Countries / Asia / 51 countries

Practice the names and locations of Asia by typing the highlighted countries.

What this Asia quiz covers

Asia stretches from the Middle East and Central Asia to India, China, Southeast Asia, and the Pacific coast. The map combines some of the world's largest countries with dense clusters of smaller neighbors.

How the typing map quiz works

One country is highlighted on the map. Type its name to answer, using the map shape and location as your clue. The round continues through all 51 countries, making this useful for spelling practice as well as map recognition.

Gameplay tip: Say each answer in your head before typing it. That small pause helps connect the written name to the map.

What you'll practice

Knowing roughly where a place is won't be enough here. You have to pull the full name from memory and spell it, which helps when your map recognition is ahead of your name recall.

A good way to start

First work out the subregion from the highlighted shape. Think of nearby places, say the answer in your head, and then type it.

When this mode helps

Try this once the shapes in Asia look familiar but some names still take a second to remember.

One mistake to watch for

Don't guess from the first letter or a vaguely similar outline. Check the highlighted area's position and neighbors first.

What makes this group challenging

Central Asia, the Caucasus, and mainland Southeast Asia contain several neighboring countries that are commonly confused. The continent also stretches across a very wide map.

Study tip

Study Asia by subregion before attempting the full quiz, especially South Asia, Central Asia, Southeast Asia, and the Middle East.
