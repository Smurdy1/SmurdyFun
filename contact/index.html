If something broke, a geography answer looks wrong, or you have an idea, this is the easiest way to tell me.

Open the feedback form

What helps in a report

  • Bug: the quiz link, what you did, and what happened.
  • Wrong answer or map data: the quiz link and a source if you have one.
  • Visual problem: a screenshot is usually the fastest way for me to understand it.
  • Idea: send it even if it is oddly specific. A lot of Smurdy is oddly specific.

Personal information

Only include what the message needs. Do not send passwords, payment details, your precise location, or other sensitive information. Google operates the form, and submissions are handled as described in the Smurdy Privacy Policy.
