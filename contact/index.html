What you can report

Bugs and technical problems

Tell me which quiz you were playing, what you did, and what went wrong.

Quiz or map corrections

If a country list, answer, label, or piece of map data looks wrong, send the quiz link and a source if you have one.

Ideas and suggestions

New regions, modes, review features, and design changes are all fair game.

Privacy questions

The same form works for questions about analytics, browser storage, ads, or submitted information.

How to make a report useful

For a bug, include the mode and region, what you expected to happen, and what happened instead. Your browser or device can matter too. Screenshots are useful when the problem is visual.

Before sending personal information

Only include what the message needs. Do not send passwords, payment details, your precise location, or other sensitive information. Google operates the form, and submissions are handled as described in the Smurdy Privacy Policy.
