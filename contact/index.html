Contact Smurdy

Found a problem, have an idea, or want to get in touch about the project? The feedback form is the main contact route for Smurdy.

Send a message

Use the Smurdy feedback form for bug reports, feature suggestions, geography corrections, general questions, or privacy requests. You can choose whether to include contact information.

Open the feedback form

What you can report

Bugs and technical problems

Report something that behaves incorrectly, looks broken, or prevents a quiz from working.

Quiz or map corrections

Point out a missing place, incorrect answer, disputed label, map issue, or confusing quiz description.

Ideas and suggestions

Suggest a quiz mode, region, learning feature, design change, or other improvement to Smurdy.

Privacy questions

Ask about site data, browser storage, analytics, advertising, or information submitted through the form.

How to make a report useful

For a technical problem, include the quiz mode and region, what you expected, what happened instead, and the device or browser you were using. A screenshot can also help. For a geography correction, include the affected quiz and a reliable source when possible.

Before sending personal information

Only include information needed for the message. Do not submit passwords, precise location information, payment details, or other sensitive personal information. The form is operated through Google, and information you submit is handled as described in the Smurdy Privacy Policy.

About the project
